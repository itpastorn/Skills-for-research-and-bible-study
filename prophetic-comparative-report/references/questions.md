# Canonical Questions

These 11 questions are always included in a prophetic comparative report, in this order. Additional questions may be added on a per-run basis based on Lars's request during clarifying questions.

Each question is answered with three sub-sections: **Agreement** (what the sources converge on), **Complementarity** (where they cover different facets without contradicting), and **Tension** (where they disagree, including Lars's own draft if it is among the sources).

## The questions

1. **What is the role of the prophetic?** What purpose does the prophetic serve in the church and in the life of the believer?

2. **Is there a difference between "the prophetic" and "hearing God's voice"?** Are these the same charism, or distinct but related modes of divine communication?

3. **How does the prophetic gift operate? And what is it not?** Mechanics, delivery, forms — and the boundary against mimicry, manipulation, or natural impression mistaken for revelation.

4. **How does God's communication relate to our body and our "spiritual senses"?** Embodiment, affect, imagination, sensory language in revelation.

5. **What is the role of covenant in prophecy (God speaking)?** Is God's speech shaped by covenant structure? How does the Old/New covenant distinction figure in?

6. **What is the difference between prophecy in the old covenant and the new?** Frequency, distribution, authority, office vs. gift, relationship to Scripture.

7. **How should prophecy be evaluated to be biblical?** The formal testing criteria drawn from Scripture (content, doctrinal alignment, community weighing per 1 Cor 14:29).

8. **How should we evaluate for fruit?** The teleological/pastoral criterion — what does the word produce over time in the recipient and the community?

9. **What about predictions? What caveats in evaluating if predictions come true?** Deut 18 vs. Jer 18, conditional and contingent prophecy, timing, vagueness, self-fulfillment, confirmation bias.

10. **How should "the Spirit of Prophecy" / inner resonance be evaluated?** The Acts-framing of prophecy as the Spirit-saturated witness of Jesus. Inner witness, "it bears witness with our spirit," and the risk of collapsing this into private emotion.

11. **What other criteria for evaluating prophecy should be mentioned?** Catch-all for criteria not covered above — e.g. character of the messenger, process of delivery, pastoral protocol, submission to authority structures, role of the wider church's reception, historical test.

## On Q12

Q12 from the Westhoff-Gunther prototype ("What is the learning curve for integrating this author's framework into ordinary congregational practice?") is **not** a canonical question. The open clarifying question about additional/custom questions covers that territory more flexibly — it can be reintroduced case by case, or replaced with something else Lars cares about for that particular comparison.
