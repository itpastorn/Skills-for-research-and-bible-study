# Report Structure Template

This is the skeleton for every prophetic comparative report. Follow it exactly. The report is written in markdown and then converted to .docx via `scripts/build_docx.py`.

## Register and tone

- Default: Level 3 (itpastorn.nu / deep article / book manuscript register). Level 2 is lighter; Level 4 is academic with full SBL 2 apparatus and a verification pass.
- Resonerande, theologically informed, normative where appropriate, mildly polemical where warranted.
- Prose over bullets. Bullets are allowed in **one place only**: the "Key takeaways" list under the abstract. No bullets in the Q&A body or in the longer summary.
- Name individual authors when stating positions. Do not write "one of the authors claims..." — write "Westhoff claims..." or "Gunther argues...".

## Notes on specific sections

### Authors in context
This is where influences and intellectual lineage go — mentors, theological tradition, prior works that shape the author's approach. Do not list them as bibliography entries; weave them into prose.

### Abstract + Key takeaways
The only bullet list in the body. Keep it to 5-10 items. Each bullet should be able to stand alone as a claim a reader could cite.

### Methodology
One paragraph. If Lars's draft is among the sources and its approach is more academic than the others, note it **here, once**. Do not repeat the observation in the Q&A.

### Q&A — Agreement / Complementarity / Tension
This is the heart of the report. Three sub-headings under each question, always in this order. If there is no genuine tension, write "None of note." — do not manufacture disagreement to fill the section.

### Longer summary
Prose synthesis. This is where you help Lars see how the pieces fit into a usable framework for his own book work. It's allowed — expected — to point toward the constructive gap the comparison opens up.

### Quality flag
Not marketing. Honest. If you reconstructed a chapter reference rather than verifying it, say so. If a source was thin on a topic that the question demanded, say so. This is the section Lars trusts the report by.
